Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 26, 2026

Plain-language summary: DSH Android has no HenWorks account system and includes no Firebase Analytics. Your agent workspace, API credentials, conversations, and runtime files stay in the App's private storage unless you choose a provider or feature that sends data elsewhere. Free users may receive Google AdMob ads after the applicable consent flow; verified Pro users do not request ads.

1. Scope

This policy describes how HenWorks ("we") handles information in DSH Android - Deepseek Harness ("the App"), package com.henworks.android.dsh. The App is an independent Android client and is not endorsed by or affiliated with DeepSeek.

2. Data stored on your device

The App can store agent settings, model/provider configuration, API credentials you enter, conversations, tool output, installed reviewed extensions, runtime files, consent state, and a cached Pro entitlement in the App's private storage. Android backup is disabled for the App. This local data remains until you delete it in the App, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App.

3. AI providers and extensions you choose

When you use an AI provider, the App sends the information needed to perform your request directly to the provider or endpoint you selected. That information may include your API credential, prompts, conversation context, attached content, and relevant tool results. The provider handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. HenWorks does not operate an AI inference relay for this release.

Only extensions reviewed for the Google Play build may be enabled. An extension can access data or network capabilities described before activation. Its own disclosure may apply. Do not include secrets in bug reports or other information you intentionally share.

4. Advertising and consent

The free tier uses Google AdMob. The App asks Google Play Billing to determine entitlement first and only enters the advertising path for a verified free user. Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents consent choices before an ad request. The App provides a privacy-options control when the platform requires it. A verified Pro user does not request ads.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or share an IP address (which may indicate general location), app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID or app set ID when available. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Choices and limited-ad behavior depend on your region, consent, and device settings.

5. Purchases and reviews

Google Play processes the one-time Pro purchase. We do not receive your payment-card details. The App receives product, purchase, acknowledgement, and entitlement information needed to complete or restore the purchase. Google handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy. If you choose to open the Google Play review flow, Google processes that interaction.

7. Security and retention

App data is kept in Android private storage, and supported remote connections use encrypted transport when the selected service provides HTTPS/TLS. No system can be guaranteed completely secure. HenWorks retains no App account profile or conversation database for this release. Google and the AI providers you choose retain information according to their own policies.

8. Your choices and deletion

  • Use the App's privacy-options control when it is available to review or change advertising choices.
  • Disconnect a provider or remove its credential from the App.
  • Delete conversations or runtime data using available App controls.
  • Clear App storage or uninstall the App to remove all locally stored App data.
  • Use Android's advertising privacy controls to reset or delete the advertising ID where supported.

Requests concerning data controlled by Google or an AI provider should be directed to that service. For privacy questions about HenWorks, email info@henworks.com. Do not send API keys, prompts, or other secrets by email.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age required in their country), and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a HenWorks account or analytics service.

10. International processing

Google and the AI provider you select may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy notices describe the relevant locations and safeguards.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the App's data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected in the App or its Google Play listing as appropriate.
