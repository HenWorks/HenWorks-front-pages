1. Overview

HenWorks ("we", "us", or "our") is an independent software developer based in Taiwan. We build digital tools and applications with a focus on user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information across our website (henworks.com) and all of our products.

We collect the minimum data necessary to provide our services. We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

2. How to Read This Policy

This single policy covers every HenWorks product. Because our products differ — some run entirely on your device, others require an account and cloud sync — the specifics are set out per product in the table in Section 3.

  • Sections 4–12 describe practices, third parties and rights that apply across our products.
  • Section 3 tells you which of those apply to the product you are using.

If a statement in Section 3 conflicts with a general statement elsewhere in this policy, Section 3 governs for that product.

4. This Website (henworks.com)

Our website does not use Google Analytics or any third-party analytics platform. We do not track individual visitors or use fingerprinting technologies.

Standard server access logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamp) may be retained by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) for security and performance purposes. These logs are not used to identify individual users and are subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Cookies

henworks.com uses only essential functional cookies (for example, language preference). Google AdSense units served on this site may set advertising cookies; see Section 6.

5.2 What is outside this list

Section 5.1 is exhaustive. A category not listed there is not collected by any product covered by Section 3. In particular:

  • We do not collect precise or approximate location.
  • We do not read your contacts, calendar, photos, messages, or files beyond what you explicitly select within a product for that product's stated purpose.
  • We operate no crash-reporting SDK. Where crash and performance data reaches us at all, it is aggregated data supplied by the app store, not data our software collects.
  • Analytics is not universal. Only a product whose row in Section 3 names an analytics service uses one; every other product collects no usage statistics at all.
  • Camera access, where a product requests it, is used only for the stated in-product purpose (for example, scanning a pairing QR code); images are not stored or transmitted.

5.3 Data that stays on your device

Credentials you enter for third-party services — including API keys — are written to that app's private storage on your device and are never transmitted to HenWorks. We operate no server that receives them and have no technical means to read them. The same applies to your conversations, session history, saved memories, and any AI models you download for on-device use.

Some products include servers that listen only on your own device, or on your local network at your explicit request — for example a pairing endpoint, or a local inference endpoint. These are not reachable from the internet by us, and we receive nothing through them.

5.4 Transmissions you initiate

Some products let you connect a third-party service of your own choosing — for example, an AI provider you already hold an account or API key with. When you use such a feature, the content you submit is transmitted directly from your device to that provider, authenticated with your own credentials. HenWorks does not operate, proxy, intercept, or log that traffic, and we receive no copy of it.

That provider's handling of your data is governed by their privacy policy, not this one. Please review it before connecting a service.

Similarly, when a product downloads a file you asked for from a public source — for example a model repository or a software release page — that source can see your IP address, as it would for any ordinary download.

6. Advertising

Some of our products are supported by advertising. Section 3 states which ones, and which network each uses.

Google AdMob (Android apps)

Our advertising-supported Android apps use Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and advertisement impressions and clicks, and may share that data with Google and its advertising partners in order to serve and measure ads. These apps declare the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission for this purpose.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, and opt out of personalised advertising, in your Android device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Where an app offers a paid option that removes advertising, purchasing it stops advertisements from being requested or displayed in that app.

Google AdSense (website and in-app WebViews)

henworks.com, and in-app WebViews that load content from henworks.com, may display Google AdSense units. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

You may opt out of personalised advertising at Ads Settings. For more information on how Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We may also display self-hosted advertisements and affiliate links for third-party products and services. These are labelled "Recommended" or "Sponsored". We may receive a commission if you make a purchase through them.

8. Retention and Deletion

Data that stays on your device (Section 5.3) is under your control. Uninstalling a product, or using any delete function it provides, removes it. We cannot delete it on your behalf, because we never received it.

For products that require an account, you may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing hello@henworks.com. We will action the request within 30 days.

Data held by the third parties listed in Section 7 is retained under their own policies. You can reset your advertising identifier at any time in your device settings, which severs the link between past advertising data and your device.
